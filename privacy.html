Privacy Policy
Last Updated: June 2026

At Divine Express, we take your personal data security seriously. This policy describes how we collect, store, share, and safeguard your personal data details when visiting our storefront or booking cargo operations.

🔒 1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Profile Identifiers: Name, physical residential shipping addresses, telephone contact numbers, and email accounts details supplied during registration.
  • Freight Compliance Metadata: In line with national border protection systems, we securely index Taxpayer Registration Numbers (TRN) and legal identification manifests strictly to execute destination clearance protocols.

🛠️ 2. How Your Information Is Used

  • To securely process financial billing variables and print package logistics freight routing routing sheets.
  • To keep you updated on cargo tracking events, custom clearances checkpoints, or delivery estimates via SMS or electronic mail.
  • To safeguard our checkout networks from localized payment frauds or bot security disruptions.

🤝 3. Data Protection & Sharing

  • No Commercial Selling: We do not sell or trade customer details data structures to corporate brokers or advertising tracking brokers.
  • Legal Intermediaries: Your shipment records are shared strictly with verified transit processing intermediaries (e.g., shipping freight lines, Jamaica Customs Agency port databases) exclusively to fulfill transit clearance steps.
