Shipping Rules & Prohibited Items List
CRITICAL WARNING: Packing prohibited items inside your shipments can result in immediate border seizures, heavy fines from maritime authorities, or complete legal penalties. Please check your contents thoroughly.

🇬🇧 ➡️ 🇯🇲 UK to Jamaica Route (Strictly Prohibited Goods)

The following items MUST NOT be packed inside your personal boxes, containers, or barrels:

  • Weapons & Tactical Gear: Firearms, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, radar detectors, toy/replica guns, swords, camouflage clothing, or tactical military gear (strictly penalized under Jamaican law).
  • Hazardous & Flammable Liquids: Pressurized aerosol cans (spray deodorants, hairsprays, spray paints), perfumes/colognes, lithium batteries (unless safely installed inside an electronic device), and corrosive cleaning chemicals.
  • Perishable Meats & Soil: Fresh, frozen, or uncured meats, live plants, seeds, and soil—unless you possess an explicit import permit from the Jamaican Ministry of Agriculture.
  • Illicit Substances: Narcotics, controlled drugs, and drug paraphernalia.

🇯🇲 ➡️ 🌐 Jamaica to World Route (Restricted Global Exports)

When fulfilling grocery orders and exporting shipments out of Jamaica, the following regulations apply:

  • Alcohol Limits: High-proof spirits (like overproof rum) are strictly regulated and capped based on the destination country's legal customs thresholds.
  • Uncertified Goods: Any unsealed raw meat, fresh ground produce, or non-commercial crops are entirely banned from global cargo freight networks due to strict biosecurity regulations.
