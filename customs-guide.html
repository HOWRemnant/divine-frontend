Global Shipping & Customs Compliance Guide
Important Notice for both our "Jamaica to World" and "UK to Jamaica" Clients:
Because Divine Express operates two distinct shipping pipelines, compliance with international border customs is critical. Please review the rules below depending on your shipping route.

🇬🇧 ➡️ 🇯🇲 Route 1: UK to Jamaica (Boxes, Barrels & Personal Cargo)

When packing your custom Small, Medium, Large Box, or Jumbo Plastic Barrel at our UK depot for delivery into Jamaica, you must strictly comply with the Jamaica Customs Agency regulations.

  • Commercial Quantities: Shipping large quantities of a single identical item (e.g., 30 bottles of the same body wash or 40 packs of the same underwear) will cause Jamaica Customs to classify your container as commercial trade rather than personal effects. This results in heavy commercial import duties.
  • Invoicing: Keep your purchase receipts handy. If customs officials dispute the declared value of items inside your box/barrel, matching store receipts are required to clear the hold.

🇯🇲 ➡️ 🌐 Route 2: Jamaica to World (JM Shop Orders)

When purchasing authentic Jamaican food, staples, and sweets from our JM Shop for delivery to the UK, USA, or globally, all products are screened to meet international import standards (such as UK Border Force and US FDA regulations).

  • Unprocessed Agricultural Items: We only ship commercially manufactured, sealed, and packaged grocery goods (e.g., Grace, Walkerswood, National). Raw, unsealed, or home-dried agricultural produce cannot be shipped due to international biosecurity laws.
