About Divine Express

Divine Express is your trusted Jamaican concierge shopping and shipping service — connecting you to the authentic products, flavours, and essentials you love from home. Whether you're abroad missing Yard or in the UK sending items back to Jamaica, we make the process fast, simple, and stress‑free.

Our mission is simple: bring home closer to you. From groceries and beauty products to clothing, gifts, and hard‑to‑find Jamaican favourites, we source, shop, and ship with care — delivering straight to your door worldwide.
