2. Divine Express — Refund & Order Cancellation Policy Divine Express — Refund & Cancellation Policy Last Updated: June 2026 At Divine Express, we take great pride in bringing home closer to you. Because we operate both an e-commerce grocery store and an international freight shipping line, our refund rules vary by service type: 🛒 1. JM Shop (Jamaica to World) & Grocery Orders Perishable Items & Packaged Food: Due to health, safety, and international food import regulations, we cannot accept returns or offer refunds on grocery products, snacks, spices, or canned items once they have been dispatched from our facility. Damaged or Missing Items: If an e-commerce item arrives broken, leaking, or damaged during transit, or if an item is missing from your parcel, you must notify our customer support team within 48 hours of delivery. Please supply your order number alongside clear photographic evidence of the damaged product and outer packaging to receive a replacement or digital store credit. Out of Stock Items: If an item you ordered goes out of stock unexpectedly before dispatch, we will issue an immediate refund for that specific item or offer a comparable substitute. 📦 2. UK to Jamaica Cargo Services (Boxes & Barrels) Order/Booking Cancellations: You can cancel your shipping box or barrel container booking within 24 hours of purchase for a full refund, provided your physical container has not yet been processed, custom-packed, or manifested at our UK depot. Freight & Concierge Fees: Standard concierge handling fees (£25.00) and shipping freight costs are non-refundable once the container has been securely sealed and cleared for sea or air transit pipelines. Customs Delays & Determinations: Divine Express acts as your shipping freight forwarder, but we do not have control over local border authorities. Refunds, partial refunds, or shipping adjustments will not be issued for transit delays caused directly by extensive Jamaica Customs Agency examinations, port congestion, or customs clearance holds.
